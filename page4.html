#114. 2-0: La Juventus de Spalletti toca fondo ante el Genoa

📅 Oct 28, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Serie A - Jornada 9 - 28/10/2026 20:45 CET

La Juventus de Turín cayó de mala manera ante el Genoa en el Luigi Ferraris (2-0) tras completar una flojísima actuación y sigue perdiendo comba respecto a la parte alta de la Serie A. Los de Luciano Spalleti firmaron un primer tiempo desastroso en el que encajaron los dos goles y después fueron incapaces de reaccionar de poner en apuros al cuadro genovés, que solo sufrió con un tiro en el larguero de Koppmeiners ya en el tiempo de descuento.

La Juventus no encuentra la regularidad en el juego ni en los resultados (cuatro puntos cosechados de los últimos 12) y prueba de ello fue su puesta en escena en Génova, condicionada en parte por el pelotón de bajas que arrastra. Incapaz de dominar a su rival desde la posesión o la presión, el conjunto bianconero sufrió un mundo para contener los contragolpes de los atacantes locales, que encontraron un latifundio a la espalda de Bremer, Lucumí y Gatti.

El encuentro se puso cuesta arriba desde el inicio. Un centro raso de Baldanzi desde la izquierda se paseó por el área hasta caer en los pies de Ehizibue en el segundo palo. El carrilero alemán disparó con fuerza y ni Cambiaso ni Vicario pudieron impedir que el balón entrara en la meta juventina (12').

Lejos de reaccionar, los de Spalletti se fueron deshaciendo con el paso de los minutos. Al filo de la media hora, un grave error de Kalulu siendo último hombre lo aprovechó Vitinha para recuperar el balón y enfilar el área de Vicario. El delantero no solucionó bien la oportunidad y perdió el balón, pero apareció por detrás Baldanzi para rebañarlo y subir el 2-0 al marcador (29').

Antes del descanso, el propio Kalulu tuvo en sus botas la oportunidad de reducir distancias pero, solo en el punto de penalti, envió el balón muy desviado. Tras el paso por los vestuarios, Spalletti movió ficha dando entrada a Zhegrova y Woltemade pero ni por esas la Juve pudo amenazar a Biljow, bien protegido por su defensa. Solo al final, y sin nada que perder, tuvieron los bianconeri dos opciones de hacer el 2-1, pero ni por esas.

Una Juve cada vez más extraviada cayó sin remedio ante un Genoa que celebró por todo lo alto un triunfo que le aleja de los puestos de descenso. Los de Spalleti caen a la octava posición, siguen fuera de puestos de acceso a Europa y se distancian hasta los 11 puntos de los líderes Inter y Lazio.

#31. Nketiah takes Crystal Palace past Fulham and earns Sage breathing space

📅 Oct 28, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

“Can we play you every week?” chanted the Crystal Palace fans as they celebrated a fifth victory over Fulham on their own patch in the space of 18 months. Eddie Nketiah’s first-half goal against the run of play eased some of the growing pressure on Pierre Sage as Palace booked a third consecutive appearance in the quarter-finals of this competition.

The good news for Sage is that Arsenal – who beat them on both of those occasions – have already been eliminated. However, he knows that Saturday’s game at Tottenham will have much more bearing on whether he is still around by the time Palace’s Carabao Cup campaign resumes against Sunderland in mid-December.

For Álvaro Arbeloa, this was a disappointing setback after a couple of weeks of progress. Fulham have not beaten Palace at Craven Cottage since January 2005 – a run of 10 matches – and will not get a better chance after Sage made 10 changes, including the inspired return of their captain, Dean Henderson, in goal. His save in stoppage time from Gonzalo García proved crucial and there were boos for Fulham at the final whistle. Arbeloa will be relieved that at least he does not have to face Palace again this season.

Sage left the pitch blowing kisses in the direction of his wife in the stands, but praised the travelling supporters for helping his team get over the line. “I like the fans, but I love my wife,” he said. “All the fans are really behind us – they are happy when the team is like this. The connection is better when we fight on the pitch.”

Fortunes have contrasted significantly for these two sides since Palace’s 3-2 win here at the start of September. Successive 1-0 victories against Hull and Coventry in the Premier League took some of the heat off Arbeloa but he was disappointed that a rotated side, which still contained several key players, could not take their chances – an issue becoming a recurring theme under the Spaniard. “It’s the same story – we need to learn to be more clinical,” said Arbeloa. “It’s a really frustrating night for everyone.”

Ties will take place during the week commencing 14 December

Sage had no choice but to rotate his side heavily after their exertions last week, which included travelling to Istanbul for a draw with Beskitas in the Europa League before losing to Newcastle at home three days later. No doubt with an eye on Saturday’s trip to north London that could define the Frenchman’s future, Chris Richards was the only player to retain his place. There has been talk of a protest before Palace’s next home game against Liverpool over rising ticket prices and what some supporters have described as the club’s strategy “which prioritises tourists instead of regular match-going fans” by playing loud music before kick-off and half-time. The fans who packed the away section were in full voice throughout, even as their second-string struggled against opponents who were clearly brimming with confidence.

Fulham went close twice in the space of 60 seconds when Kenny Tete headed wide from a corner before Henderson – after month on the sidelines – saved well with his feet from Antonee Robinson at the far post. Joachim Andersen should have done better with the next chance from another corner soon after, but the Denmark defender could not hit the target against his former club.

With Cheick Doucouré making his first senior appearance in midfield since January 2025 after a litany of injury problems, Palace looked rusty all over the pitch and did not manage a single touch in Fulham’s box during the first half-hour, amassing just 22% of possession. But they still managed to take the lead when Óscar Mingueza found space down the right flank and delivered an inch-perfect cross for Nketiah to volley in expertly. After all their dominance, Fulham’s supporters could hardly believe their eyes.

Palace should have been punished when Honest Ahanor gave the ball away on the edge of his area but Rodrigo Muniz’s shot was weak. Arbeloa turned to his bench and there were huge cheers for Josh King’s arrival. But Jefferson Lerma and Henderson somehow managed to clear Iwobi’s header off the line as Fulham finally created a chance their pressure merited.

The Palace substitutes Daichi Kamada and Dwight McNeil could have sealed the victory with late chances as Fulham pushed forward in desperation for an equaliser. Their supporters could not hide their frustration at full-time as Palace’s sang about another possible trip to Wembley. What a difference a few days make.

#64. Kompany discharged from hospital following collapse

📅 Oct 28, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Kompany was appointed Bayern Munich manager in May 2024

Bayern Munich head coach Vincent Kompany has been discharged from hospital and is "doing well" following his collapse at the club's training ground.

Kompany, 40, was rushed to hospital last Friday after taking ill as he watched the club's second team train.

The cause has been identified as meningeal irritation - an inflammation of the protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord.

"Given the circumstances, he's doing well," Max Eberl, the club's board member for sports, told German broadcaster ARD on Wednesday.

"He was discharged from the hospital today. That is the biggest and best news for all of us."

No timeline has been set for Kompany's return and Bayern said on Saturday that it would be a matter of "weeks, not days" until the former Manchester City defender returned to the dugout.

English assistant coach Aaron Danks has taken over in Kompany's absence and oversaw a 3-1 win at Freiburg on Sunday.

Kompany had been nursing a virus in the days before his collapse and missed some team training sessions.

The Belgian missed a pre-match news conference prior to Bayern's Champions League game against Arsenal last Tuesday, but was subsequently on the touchline for the match on Wednesday.

"It's a meningeal irritation that caused this incident on Friday. He will now rest and take medication," said Eberl, speaking before Bayern's German Cup tie against Magdeburg.

"I am very confident that he will be back on the touchline, operating at 100% very soon."

Current leaders Bayern face title rivals Borussia Dortmund in the Bundesliga on Saturday.

#121. Los socios del Athletic vuelven a respaldar a Uriarte

📅 Oct 28, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Jon Uriarte volvió a superar con suficiencia el examen de la Asamblea Ordinaria. El segundo mandato arranca de la misma manera en la que transcurrieron los cuatro años de la primera legislatura. En plena semana de derbi y a poco más de 24 horas de estrenarse en Copa, los compromisarios del Athletic dieron el visto bueno a la gestión de la junta directiva y a las cuentas del pasado ejercicio y también al presupuesto de la 2026-27. En todos ellos con un respaldo de, como mínimo, el 58 % de los socios presentes en el Palacio Euskalduna.

El resultado más ajustado estuvo precisamente en la valoración sobre la gestión. Hubo un ligero “tirón de orejas” al equipo de Uriarte, aunque recibió 329 votos afirmativos sobre un total de 567. Es decir, una aprobación del 58 %. 210 socios les dijeron que no (37 %) y 28 votaron en blanco (5 %). Seguramente aquí queda reflejado el rendimiento deportivo de los dos primeros equipos del Athletic, ni el masculino ni el femenino cumplieron con sus objetivos.

Con lo referente a las cuentas de la temporada 2025-26 el respaldo fue mayoritario. 421 socios rojiblancos votaron que sí, de un total de 530 sufragios emitidos (79 %). 98 reprobaron a la junta (19 %) y 11 fueron en blanco (2 %).
