#365. La última Copa de Iago Aspas

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

El capitán comienza a despedirse del torneo

El celtismo siempre ha soñado con que Iago Aspas levantase un título para el Celta y que el camino menos complejo para conseguirlo era la Copa del Rey. En esta temporada de despedida, el capitán arranca su última participación en este torneo.

Desconociendo si Claudio Giráldez le dará descanso en el partido de este miércoles en Salamanca, el delantero ha disputado, hasta la fecha, 43 encuentros en el torneo en el que ha marcado 19 goles y dado 8 asistencias.

En dos ocasiones ha alcanzado las semifinales. En ambas con el Celta, en las temporadas 2015-16 y 2016-17. Superar esa penúltima ronda sería el gran y complejo reto para esta temporada.

La campaña en la que más goles marcó en la Copa del Rey fue curiosamente el año que jugó en Sevilla, edición en la que hizo siete tantos. Los siete últimos años no vio puerta en el torneo porque su participación fue inferior.

Desde que es futbolista profesional ha dispuesto de minutos en todos los torneos de Copa del Rey, obviamente a excepción del año que jugó en Liverpool. En total, lleva dieciséis participaciones en el torneo.

#184. Mourinho’s plan comes together as Real Madrid relaunch with clásico statement | Sid Lowe

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

It was just like old times as José Mourinho ended a night at Camp Nou with a knee slide and three points

José Mourinho had already done the por qué?, the printout and the press conference no-show in under 100 days. Now he’s done a knee slide too, and in the perfect place: back where it all began and where it can all begin again. When Kylian Mbappé scored the first in the clásico on Sunday night, Real Madrid’s manager hardly moved, sitting in the shadow of the bench – but when the striker got the second to win it with time running out, that was different. Mourinho leapt into the light, throwing himself to the grass, the knees of a 63-year-old’s trousers muddy as a schoolboy’s, stuck his tongue out and pumped his fists.

He does so love it when a plan comes together. To the right of him, the bench was emptying, subs joining the players in sprinting towards the south-west corner. As they leapt about, dodging the bottles thrown towards them from the end, way, way up in the furthest corner and stuck inside a glass box like a bunch of David Blaines, the small group of Madrid fans who had come were going mad. In the main stand, a thousand phones were raised. And 600 kilometres west, back in Boadilla, Thibaut Courtois was watching his teammates from the sofa at home, presumably wrapped in a blanket and cradling a cup of Lemsip. On the morning of the biggest game there is, he awoke with flu, forced to miss out.

“We have the best goalkeeper in the world, but none of us cried because Thibaut couldn’t play,” Mourinho said. Plenty might have felt like it, more reason to be fearful. But the coach called Andriy Lunin, the substitute goalkeeper so serious that he bought a roll of artificial turf and spent the pandemic diving round the living room of his flat, and told him this was his moment: “You train to play, not to sit on the bench, so go on.” As for the rest of them, the manager told them nothing changes: the plan remains in place. By the time they headed back to the dressing room, “Fucking get in!” echoing along the Camp Nou corridor, so it had proved.

Thirteen years later, Mourinho was back in Barcelona for a clásico and he had guided Madrid to a 2-1 win. It wasn’t supposed to be like this, or maybe it was. Barcelona, the team who had won nine in a row, banging in 4.3 goals per game, had been beaten by Madrid, the team who had lost twice already and, truth be told, were mostly just not very good. The night it could have been all over, the title race done when it’s not even in November, was instead the night it finally started. “If you leave here nine points behind, it’s very heavy psychologically; if it’s six, it’s more balanced; if it’s three, that’s different,” Mourinho said.

This was different, and so is the season now. Counting the clásicos all those years ago, Mourinho racked up his sixth consecutive meeting with Barcelona without defeat, and there had been no parked bus, nor many lumps kicked. They didn’t just desperately hang on, although there was a moment when Dean Huijsen saved a shot with his face. Nor had they just waited for a moment, resisting and then robbing it, even if João Cancelo did insist: “That’s Madrid; they don’t have many chances but they score them.” The final shot count said 18-10 to Barcelona, and possession was 62% Barcelona’s.

“The way we won is important: you can win luckily or by defending a lot. But we won with calmness and totally deservedly. We did it by being organised, concentrated and having the courage to come out from the back,” Mourinho said. And if that might have been pushing it a little – and, let’s face it, the analyses might be different had Lunin not made an astonishing save from Gerard Martín or Lamine Yamal’s second-half shot not faded just past the post – he had a point.

Barcelona were not at their best. They had been hurried and imprecise, Rodri telling his teammates to lower their “pulse rate” as they came out for the second half. Lamine Yamal somehow missed an extraordinary chance just before the break. And when they did tighten the screw in the second half, they couldn’t break the wall. Xavi Espart was forced off, yet another injury at the back, the failure to sign a centre-back in the spotlight again. Yet this was still a faster, more aggressive, tighter Madrid than anyone expected. Asked to define it, Lunin went for “discipline and attitude”. And if that is partly about occasion, the least you can ask for, it’s more than you sometimes get.

Besides, it was also more than that. More than Marc Cucurella getting under Lamine Yamal’s skin, although he certainly succeeded. They went for the space beyond Barcelona’s full-backs: superb in his first clásico, aged 19, Yan Diomandé – the left winger – got more touches than any Madrid player, and protected his full-back as well. But it was more than that, too. More than the wonderful winning goal, a flash of inspiration with Jude Bellingham going all Nureyev, the ball flitting between his feet and Gabriel Jesus’s legs, pirouetting to release Eduardo Camavinga. This was a team with an idea and a victory that vindicated their manager, some kind of resurrection.

Before the game, Rodri said that one of Madrid’s tricks is to convince you that they’re not that good, but insisted he wouldn’t fall into that trap. Still he felt that plan particularly. Pedri did too. Bellingham dealt with Rodri, Bernardo Silva with Pedri. Pressed and tired too, according to Hansi Flick, neither truly took control. The inclusion of Arda Güler and Silva – the surprise in the middle, starting his first league game in six weeks – underlined an intention for Madrid to keep the ball when they could. They actually had a midfield, which was new. From the back they tried to build, draw Barcelona in. And if a time came when they had to wait, to put bodies in before the ball, well, they would do that too. Six times Barcelona shots were blocked. At least twice more, shots were taken from their toes. At the other end, meanwhile, Madrid attacked with an intent and an incision that probably drew the clearer chances: the xG showed 1.19 v 1.94.

Victory puts Madrid three points behind Barcelona and, Federico Valverde claimed, “will give us a kickstart”. It has taken 10 weeks but it’s game on. Hay liga, as they like to say. There is league. There is Madrid, too. Something has shifted, starting in Rome, where 70 dreadful minutes gave way to 20 pretty good ones, another inexplicable comeback actually having something of an explanation, too. The changes were impossible to ignore. Valverde and Aurélien Tchouaméni no longer the partnership in the middle, Valverde going to right-back instead. Güler playing. Diomandé playing. And, yes, Vinícius Júnior not playing. “I’m not stubborn; I can change,” Mourinho said. Madrid have won five in succession now; something was seen that night, a way ahead. They’ve won every game since Vinícius was suspended, facile and perhaps even cruel though that may be to say. And since Mourinho grew a beard, if you believe in that kind of thing.

Before the clásico, Mourinho had said it wasn’t fair to compare his team, a side in construction and a long way from the finished product, to Barcelona. He insisted that he was still getting to know his players; he hadn’t been there long, after all. After the clásico, he admitted: “We have improved, we can’t hide that.” The idea has shifted, almost like it was an epiphany: “I’ve learned something about the middle of the pitch; I have learned that a mixture is better, that it is better to have a combination of qualities than two players of similar characteristics.” In short, Tchouaméni and Valverde doesn’t work. There needs to be some football in there somewhere, and so a shift started. Sunday came as a kind of confirmation, a hope, an identity emerging.

“The best Madrid is born at the Camp Nou,” ran the headline in El País. “Madrid are here now,” AS’s cover said. “I won’t let them have their heads on the moon; if we win this and then don’t win at the Sardinero, it won’t mean much,” Mourinho said, but this was a start, not just an end in itself. At full time, Mourinho headed on to the pitch and shook Lunin’s hand as all around players embraced. High in the north-east corner, Madrid’s fans stayed and sang. Back home, Courtois was watching. He had “a horrible night” on Saturday, they said, but now he was feeling a little better. They all were.

#303. Why is Wilcox in line of fire from Man Utd fans?

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Jason Wilcox (fourth from left) watched United's 3-0 defeat by Bournemouth alongside chief executive Omar Berrada

As the clock ticked down during Manchester United's 3-0 defeat by Bournemouth at Old Trafford on Sunday, the home fans made it clear what they thought about director of football Jason Wilcox.

Singing his name along with an obscenity, the message was brutal and to the point. It was sung by a majority of the United supporters, and on more than one occasion.

It came less than two weeks after United defender Lisandro Martinez had launched a blistering attack on the club's hierarchy for not strengthening the squad, with a particular message for Wilcox.

"I expect more from our technical director," said Martinez.

Pressure is growing on United boss Michael Carrick after eight games without a win - their worst run since 2015-16 - and two three-goal defeats in a week.

Wilcox is a former England winger who won the Premier League with Blackburn in 1994-95.

He joined United in April 2024 - initially as technical director after 15 months in a similar role at Manchester City, where he had previously been in charge of the academy.

At City he worked with current United chief executive Omar Berrada and also Stephen Torpey, who was appointed United's head of academy in August 2025.

When he arrived, it was said by United officials that Wilcox would work across all technical areas of the club to achieve "highest standards of performance" and would be answerable to the sporting director.

That changed the following December, when that sporting director - Dan Ashworth - was sacked amid a difference of opinion over who should replace Erik ten Hag as manager.

Ten months on, a poor series of results and dissatisfaction with transfer business has put Wilcox in the spotlight.

There are some who have sympathy with the position Wilcox finds himself in. The 55-year-old is not in charge of transfer budgets, which are set at a higher level.

Wilcox was also - obviously - not responsible for the injury Manuel Ugarte sustained playing for Uruguay at the World Cup, which wrecked a plan to sell the midfielder in the summer to bolster the transfer budget.

He, too, was powerless to prevent Barcelona backing out of a Â£26m deal to sign England forward Marcus Rashford.

Both issues affected the amount United could spend in the summer - both on transfer fees and wages.

Wilcox rarely speaks to the media, although in England that is not uncommon when managers do the vast majority of news conferences.

However a general lack of communication means we don't know why United decided to focus exclusively on strengthening their midfield in the summer, rather than bring in competition at left-back for Luke Shaw and striking support for Benjamin Sesko.

With both players injured, that looks like a major error.

The general consensus is Carrick has been sold badly short, and Wilcox's job title - and the fact he is often photographed with United's new signings and quoted in official press releases - means he is being blamed.

Since arriving at United from Southampton, Wilcox has twice been given very public backing by his bosses.

On the first occasion - around Ruben Amorim's recruitment as successor to Ten Hag - it was Ashworth who lost his job while Wilcox kept his.

Thirteen months later, when Amorim railed publicly against interference around United's formation - identifying Wilcox as one of those responsible - the Portuguese coach went, and Wilcox remained.

Minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe is yet to offer his thoughts on the present crisis engulfing Old Trafford, but the United fans delivered their verdict loud and clear during the match against Bournemouth.

There are many reasons why United find themselves 16th in the Premier League and without a win in their past eight matches, but recruitment is one of the bigger ones - and for that, rightly or wrongly, Wilcox is getting the blame.

Since joining United, Wilcox has given one extensive interview - to the in-house Inside United podcast in October 2025.

A month before that, he spoke at the 40th anniversary gala dinner of United's past players' association.

There have been a couple of instances in the past 12 months of him stopping for the briefest of chats with journalists when he walked past an area they were waiting in to speak to a United player after a game.

After the defeat by Bournemouth, Carrick was asked directly if Wilcox and Berrada should speak directly to supporters.

"It's not for me to say who speaks and who doesn't speak, when they speak, and how they speak," said Carrick.

"I know my role here is to speak to you guys and represent the team and football club as best as I possibly can from this position."

There has never been an opportunity to ask Wilcox about the parameters of his role.

But with Carrick's situation as it is - his popularity intact and his status as a legendary figure at the club secure - the focus is moving to Wilcox.

#373. Cuatro de cada cinco solicitantes de entradas para el derbi no estarán en San Mamés

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

La Real ha comunicado los 455 afortunados por sorteo de entre las 2.270 peticiones

Cuatro de cada cinco solicitantes de las entradas para el derbi del próximo domingo en San Mamés no podrán estar en las gradas del campo bilbaíno, al haber sido 2.270 las peticiones que la Real Sociedad ha recibido de sus socios y RS Fans y tener sólo 455 localidades para ser sorteadas.

Las personas solicitantes han recibido un correo electrónico en el que se les informa del resultado del sorteo. Las que han sido agraciadas deberán acceder al portal del abonado en un máximo de 24 horas desde que han recibido esa comunicación electrónico para completar la compra. En caso de no realizar el pago dentro de ese plazo, perderán el derecho a las entradas. Una vez completado el pago, recibirán las localidades por correo electrónico con al menos 72 horas de antelación al encuentro.

A las personas no agraciadas se les devolverá en los próximos días el adelanto de 10 euros para participar en el sorteo, mediante un reembolso en la misma tarjeta utilizada para realizar el pago. Las entradas serán nominativas y no se permitirá el acceso al estadio a ninguna persona diferente de aquella a la que esté asignada cada localidad.

Además de esas 455 personas afortunadas en el sorteo, la Real estará apoyada por al menos 120 más, por las entradas que destinó a compromisos del club y peñas, y a buen seguro que una vez más la buena relación entre ambas aficiones se hace notar y son algunos más de esos 575 realistas los presentes en San Mamés, al conseguir algún carnet de un muy buen amigo vizcaíno.

#321. The horse that ushered in Manchester United’s tragic arc | Jonathan Wilson

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

There’s plenty of blame to go around at Old Trafford. Like all downfalls it started with a seemingly small, unconnected moment

And so here we are again, at that point as the nights draw in and Manchester United prepare to sack a manager. Autumn: season of mists, mellow fruitfulness and a crisis at Old Trafford. Sunday’s 3-0 home defeat to Bournemouth, coming on the back of last week’s 4-1 reverse against Como, made clear what many had feared from the moment Michael Carrick was given the job on a permanent basis: that he isn’t up to it.

But then, who could be? When you’ve burned through a long list of managers in 13 years, it’s perhaps worth considering whether the manager is really the problem. Could some blame perhaps go to the director of football, Jason Wilcox, or the chief executive, Omar Berrada, the people making the appointments? Or perhaps to Sir Jim Ratcliffe, the cost-cutting tax exile and minority shareholder with responsibility for sporting operations? Or to the Glazer family, who plunged the club into debt with a leveraged buyout in 2005 and have since made calamitous decision after calamitous decision, reducing the most successful club in English league history to a mid-table laughing stock with a roof that leaks symbolically?

It’s worth remembering where all this began. The United story has taken on the aspect of a classical tragedy and, like all good tragedies, it begins with a small, seemingly unconnected moment, amplified by pride, that ends up bringing the whole edifice crashing down.

In 1999, Mull of Kintyre, a horse owned by the Irish millionaire John Magnier, won the Gimcrack Stakes at York. Traditionally, the winning owner would give a speech at the Gimcrack Dinner, but Magnier was a shy man who hated public speaking so he asked his friend, the United manager Alex Ferguson, to give the speech on his behalf. The racing establishment, though, refused: it had to be the owner. To spite them, Magnier named Ferguson as the owner of another horse, Juniper. The following year, Juniper was narrowly beaten in the Gimcrack Stakes. Magnier, though, was determined. So the year after that, he made Ferguson the owner of Rock of Gibraltar, which had won its first three races. Rock of Gibraltar won the Gimcrack easily and Magnier, waving a metaphorical two fingers at the racing authorities, watched Ferguson give the speech.

Magnier was largely making mischief, but he was also happy to do a favor for a friend, to give Ferguson a better experience of racing, and he benefited from the association of the horse with such a famous name. In addition, Ferguson did not get on well with the United chairman, Martin Edwards, and had a sense that football clubs were becoming good investments. Accordingly Magnier and his associate JP McManus bought shares in United. (The full story is explored by the journalist David Walsh here).

It turned out, though, that Rock of Gibraltar was a much better horse than anybody had realized, winning seven Group One races in a row. The stud fees would be worth tens of millions of pounds. Ferguson believed the verbal deal was that he could choose 50% of the prize money won by Rock of Gibraltar (about £800,000) or 50% of the stud fees, which ended up being worth tens of millions. He went for the latter. Magnier’s recollection was rather different: 5% of the prize money or one stud nomination a year.

Ferguson sued and, with his name on the ownership documents, probably would have won had it gone to trial. But then rumors started to spread about some of Ferguson’s financial dealings at United. Paid actors at United’s annual general meeting raised awkward points, then Magnier and McManus sent a list of 99 questions to the United board, which mysteriously found their way into the Daily Mail. United were highly embarrassed. Fans began to demonstrate against Magnier and McManus at race meetings. A settlement was reached, with Ferguson paid £2.5m to drop the action. But the damage was done.

Magnier and McManus had decided to sell their shares in United, and another shareholder was ready and waiting: Malcolm Glazer. Ferguson backed Glazer, who launched his leveraged buyout. Magnier and McManus sold up in 2005 for £260m, a profit of a little under £100m. Had they waited for Ratcliffe, who bought a similar stake in 2024, they would have received roughly £1bn more than that. For Ferguson, a threat had been dealt with.

The problem was that Glazer and his family would prove some of the worst club owners in English history. For 20 years, United have been addled by debt and have made consistently terrible footballing decisions. Ferguson’s team was good enough, and his personality big enough, that United won five league titles and a Champions League after the takeover, but once his genius had gone, there was nothing left.

A mismatched squad built up over seven flawed managerial appointments and a multiplicity of conflicting approaches will not readily be sorted out. Carrick is the latest victim of a crisis 21 years in the making. For the want of a horse a club was lost.

#348. Which game will Chelsea's Palmer miss after red card?

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Cole Palmer was sent off in the second half of Chelsea's 2-0 loss at Stamford Bridge

Chelsea's Cole Palmer will not miss the Blues' Premier League meeting with Manchester United on Saturday, despite being sent off in his side's last Premier League match.

Palmer was shown two yellow cards in the second half of the Blues' defeat by rivals Tottenham Hotspur on Saturday. That dismissal meant that Palmer was handed a one-match ban.

The England playmaker was shown his first caution for an off-the-ball incident involving Spurs' Mateus Fernandes. He was then shown a second yellow card for a foul on Conor Gallagher.

In most cases, this would be served in that team's next Premier League match. However, Palmer will not miss Chelsea's meeting with United at Stamford Bridge.

Instead, Palmer will be banned for the Blues' EFL Cup fourth-round match away at Liverpool on Wednesday night.

This is because red card suspensions are carried out across domestic competitions rather than being specific to the competition they were received in.

Suspensions for an accumulation of yellow cards across a campaign are served in the competition in which they are received.

So while the Blues will miss Palmer for their trip to Anfield, he will be available to face United three days after the club's cup tie on Merseyside.

#249. Nigeria: Hull City, Brentford Maintain Unbeaten Records After 1-1 Draw

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: World  |  Read Source →

Hull City and Brentford maintained their respective unbeaten home and away records in the Premier League after playing out a 1-1 draw at the MKM Stadium on Sunday.

Oli McBurnie put Hull ahead from the penalty spot in the 24th minute, but Dango Ouattara restored parity for Brentford in the 63rd minute as both sides settled for a point.

The result left Hull 10th in the table and Brentford sixth, with the visitors missing the opportunity to secure their first away victory of the campaign.

Hull took the lead after Brentford were caught trying to play out from the back. Aaron Hickey fouled Tim Iroegbunam inside the penalty area, prompting the referee to award a spot-kick.

McBurnie stepped up and converted confidently to register his first Premier League goal of the season and give the hosts an advantage they carried into the half-time break.

Brentford, however, grew into the contest after the interval and eventually found an equaliser through Ouattara.

The forward struck from close range in the 63rd minute after Igor Thiago supplied the assist, capping a period of sustained pressure from the visitors.

Brentford's equaliser was deserved on the balance of their second-half performance, but Hull responded positively and created several opportunities to reclaim the lead.

Mohamed Belloumi carved out space inside the penalty area but could only find the side-netting, while Mohamed-Ali Cho saw his low effort saved by the foot of Brentford goalkeeper Caoimhin Kelleher.

Hull continued to push for a winner, with Joe Gelhardt wasting another opportunity late in the match after being picked out in the middle of the penalty area. His effort drifted wide, allowing Brentford to escape with a share of the spoils.

The draw extended Hull's run without a home league victory to three consecutive matches, although they remain unbeaten on their own ground in the Premier League.

The hosts have now gone six matches without a win after making an encouraging start to the season with victories in their opening two fixtures.

#404. El Real Madrid desarmó al Barcelona sin necesitar el balón

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Tchouaméni sostuvo el bloque con once recuperaciones y cerrando el carril central a un Pedri y un Rodri poco resolutivos

El Barcelona recibía al Real Madrid con nueve victorias en nueve jornadas y la opción de firmar un diez de diez que habría sido récord en el campeonato español. El Clásico se le torció en una jugada que ningún guion había previsto. A los 41 minutos, un despeje de Gerard Martín rebotó en Mbappé para hacer el 0-1. Esa jugada resume una noche en la que el Barcelona hizo casi todo lo que se espera de él, salvo ganar. Tuvo el 62% de los pases, jugó 181 en el último tercio por 54 del Madrid y disparó 18 veces por 10. Pero el Clásico se definió en la manera en que el equipo de Mourinho renunció al balón para quedarse con las zonas que deciden los partidos.

El Madrid se plantó en un 4-2-3-1 replegado y esperó. Sus acciones defensivas se produjeron, de media, a unos 33 metros de su portería, frente a los 46 del Barcelona. Permitió de media 17,8 pases azulgranas antes de generar una acción blanca, contra 10,2 en el caso azulgrana.

Sin presionar, el Madrid defendió el área: 30 despejes, 14 intercepciones y seis tiros bloqueados por sus defensas, cuatro de ellos de Huijsen. De los 18 disparos del Barcelona, seis acabaron en un cuerpo blanco y cinco en las manos de Lunin.

El Barcelona encontró pasillos pero no remates limpios. Con tanto tiempo para mirar el campo, dio 284 pases laterales, más del doble que los 132 del Madrid, y esa circulación no llegó a desordenar al bloque rival.

Si el bloque tuvo un centro de gravedad, ese fue Tchouaméni. Firmó 11 recuperaciones, más que ningún otro jugador del partido (Güler y Pedri llegaron a siete), y con una intercepción y cuatro entradas ganadas sumó 16 acciones recuperadoras, seis más que el siguiente.

Su territorio fue el carril central de su propio campo. Nueve de las 11 recuperaciones llegaron en campo propio, y 11 de las 30 intervenciones defensivas del Madrid en esa zona, entre recuperaciones, intercepciones y entradas, llevan su firma.

Eran recuperaciones de contención. En ocho de las once, el Barcelona no volvió a tocar el balón en los doce segundos siguientes, pero ninguna acabó en tiro. Su utilidad estaba en otra parte: cortar la circulación azulgrana justo delante de Huijsen y Konaté y devolver la pelota al Madrid con un pase limpio, porque Tchouaméni acertó 44 de sus 46 pases, el 95,7%, frente al 82,8% del conjunto.

Vio la amarilla al inicio de la segunda parte y no se encogió, ya que seis de sus once recuperaciones llegaron después, lo que demuestra su madurez e inteligencia para decidir cómo y cuándo actuar. Hubo un solo borrón. En el 79' perdió un balón y cinco segundos más tarde disparó De Jong y Huijsen bloqueó el remate.

La otra cara del ancla es un Barcelona que movió mucho el balón por el centro y lo hizo avanzar poco. Rodri (96 pases) y Pedri (88) acumularon el 27% de los pases del equipo y fueron los dos máximos generadores de entradas al último tercio, con 19 de las 43 del Barcelona. Hasta ahí, el reparto que cabía esperar de dos centrocampistas con tanto balón.

Pero les faltó verticalidad. Solo el 35,5% de los pases de Rodri y el 33,7% de los de Pedri en juego abierto ganaron terreno hacia delante, por debajo del 37,6% del Barcelona y lejos del 46,9% del Madrid. Entre los dos completaron cinco pases de más de 15 metros hacia delante.

Rodri tuvo además sus minutos de ruido: cuatro pases fallados en un mismo minuto, dos de ellos seguidos de pases del Madrid al último tercio. Su acierto en juego abierto, el 87,1%, coincide con el del equipo (87,3%), así que la clave está en hacia dónde jugaba esos pases.

Pedri dejó más dudas. Fue el jugador del Barcelona que más duelos perdió, tres. En el otro platillo, dio la asistencia del 1-1 y firmó diez entradas al último tercio. El resultado fue un ataque que se concentraba en el centro, donde esperaba Tchouaméni.

Sin balón, el Madrid tuvo una vía de salida muy clara. Lunin jugó 15 de sus 33 pases en largo, el equipo lanzó 54 balones largos y la combinación más repetida de la noche fue Cucurella-Diomande, con 17 pases completados. Cucurella firmó 14 pases hacia delante de más de 15 metros. La longitud media de pase lo resume: 19,4 metros para el Madrid, 13,7 para el Barcelona.

Ese juego directo evitó el carril central. El Madrid intentó 19 pases de entrada al último tercio por su izquierda, 18 por su derecha y solo cinco por el medio, y su principal generador de tiros fue Güler desde la banda derecha, con cinco remates y tres a puerta, aunque sus ocho centros solo encontraron destinatario una vez.

Enfrente había un Barcelona reconfigurado. Cuando Espart se lesionó, Balde ocupó el lateral izquierdo y Cancelo, que había empezado por ese costado, pasó a la derecha con una posición media a unos 64 metros de su portería, junto a Yamal. Once minutos después, el 0-1 nació por Cucurella y Diomande, el lado de un Cancelo muy adelantado.

El Madrid necesitó muy poco. Disparó diez veces, cinco a puerta, y convirtió dos de las cuatro ocasiones claras. Mbappé protagonizó tres de ellas y marcó dos. El Barcelona tuvo dos ocasiones claras y no marcó ninguna: Yamal mandó fuera la de los 44' y el cabezazo de Gerard Martín a los 66' que detuvo Lunin. Su gol llegó por otro camino. Un córner sacado en corto en el que Yamal, Pedri y Raphinha necesitaron tres toques.

El 1-2 es el mejor ejemplo del partido que fue. Gabriel Jesus, recién entrado, perdió un duelo ante Bellingham. Diez segundos después, Mbappé marcaba tras una jugada de cuatro pases y dos regates en la que Cubarsí quedaba superado por Camavinga, que había salido siete minutos antes.

Después llegó el momento de contención. El Barcelona no volvió a disparar. Tras el 1-2 sumó 56 pases por 21 del Madrid y solo 11 en el último tercio, mientras el Madrid no dio ni uno pero supo aguantar bien a un desquiciado conjunto azulgrana

#407. Iván Romero se pone a 100

📅 Oct 26, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

El delantero del Levante cumplió ante la Real Sociedad su partido número cien como jugador granota

Iván Romero ya es centenario. Este domingo en el encuentro ante la Real Sociedad, el delantero titular del Levante, cumplió su partido número cien defendiendo la camiseta azulgrana en partido oficial.

El jugador de La Solana, aterrizó en la capital del Túria en el verano 2023, procedente del Sevilla FC, aunque estuvo cedido en el CD Tenerife de Segunda División. Tras un gran año en el cuadro chicharrero, los granotas formalizaron la operación.

Desde su llegada, el atacante ha ido acumulando protagonismo temporada tras temporada y se ha ido ganando el cariño de todo el pueblo del Ciutat de Valencia. En su primera temporada, en la categoría de plata, estuvo en presente en veinte encuentros y anotó un gol, sus peores números desde su llegada al Levante.

A partir de ahí, su presencia fue en aumento y gracias a sus nueve tantos en treinta y cinco partidos, Romero consiguió el ascenso a Primera División. El curso pasado, llegó su consolidación en la categoría de oro, alcanzando siete goles en treinta y dos envites oficiales.

En esta presente temporada, el delantero se estrenó como capitán del equipo y es un fijo para Luís Castro en el esquema del equipo valenciano. En estas primeras diez jornadas de liga, ha sido titular en nueve de ellas consiguiendo un total de tres goles.
