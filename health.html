#319. Local pair’s brush with Hong Kong Open history a sign the sport is flourishing

📅 Oct 23, 2026  |  Category: Health  |  Read Source →

For a time on Thursday, Isaac Lam and Matthew Cheung topped the tournament leaderboard, offering a glimpse of what might be

There was a moment on Thursday when history looked like being made at the Link Hong Kong Open, and while forgiving greens and some generous pins meant normal service was later restored, the glimpse on offer was of a local game in rude health.

For the first time since the tournament began in 1959, two Hongkongers – Isaac Lam and Matthew Cheung – briefly sat at the top of the leaderboard at six-under-par.

By the end of the day they were far from alone and further down the field than they might have hoped, with Kim Min-kyu and Yosuke Asaji also carding 64s to create a four-way tie for fifth.
